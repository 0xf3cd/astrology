Repository structure and calculation boundaries¶

简体中文 · Project · Knowledge

Birth records, modern time resolution, requested configuration, the tropical natal chart, major aspects, text output and independent knowledge are implemented; their usage and constraints are in the birth, configuration, chart, aspect, cross-chart, result and knowledge guides. The current chart is a reproducible modern tropical, geocentric natal chart, with independent access to its underlying knowledge.

Organization¶

The intended package layout is below. Create modules as their first functionality arrives; a name in this map does not require an empty package or an extra layer.

src/astrology/
  birth.py          Original records and resolved instants
  config.py         Requested chart and tradition choices
  celestial.py      Concrete adapter to celestial-calendar
  zodiac.py         Zodiac definitions and sign classification
  houses.py         House-placement rules over supplied geometry
  aspects.py        Aspect definitions, orbs, and local motion
  chart.py          Composition and immutable chart results
  cross_aspects.py  Static comparison of supplied charts, retaining both sources
  text.py           Result tables with supplied vocabulary
  knowledge/        Concepts, sources, entries, and independent queries
tests/              Domain tests and installed-consumer tests
docs/en/            English guides and explanations
docs/zh-CN/         Matching Chinese guides and explanations

Executable definitions live with their domain. chart.py composes them and owns the success contract of a complete chart; it does not become the owner of every shared definition. Common values move to a shared module only when actual users need them. Package imports and knowledge queries must not initialize celestial.

cross_aspects.py composes existing chart results without astronomy. Shared angular/orb arithmetic remains in aspects.py; single-chart pair eligibility and local motion stay with find_aspects, separate from static cross pairing.

Knowledge and calculation remain connected by stable identifiers, described in Knowledge organization. Additional UI applications can consume the result later without moving calculation into a frontend.

Birth data and time¶

Preserve the original date, calendar, clock reading, precision, location, source, and timezone information. Store the resolved instant and the choices used to resolve it separately. An explicit offset and an IANA timezone are distinct input forms; timezone attachment alone does not detect a DST gap or choose a fold.

The current prepare_natal_input validates known time, timezone and location; it does not calculate a chart. The natal_chart entry point requires that input. Unknown time remains representable and is rejected by preparation rather than replaced with noon. Historical inputs need their own calendar and time-scale contract, including the difference between pre-1972 UT1 and modern UTC.

Astronomy and astrology¶

The adapter returns astronomical values with their units, coordinate conventions, and provenance. The astrology modules derive signs, house membership, and aspects without discarding those values.

An object’s identity also survives model selection. Mean nodes, Meeus five-term true nodes, and osculating nodes cannot silently substitute for one another. Constructed composite points retain their construction identity rather than pretending to be physical bodies at a birth instant.

Composition and failures¶

Represent positions, axes/house geometry, and astrological classifications separately. A complete natal calculation may require all requested components; future partial-chart entry points must state their own success contract.

celestial v0.8.0 provides a strict fourteen-object snapshot with rates and houses. The adapter uses it within its joint domain. Selecting seven output objects does not widen that domain. Genuine independent positions and house queries are candidate celestial v0.10.0 work.

If requested Placidus geometry is unavailable, the adapter reports that result explicitly and never falls back. Any future fallback requires an explicit policy and must record both requested and effective systems. Equal and Whole Sign also retain the upstream restrictions on undefined axes; they are not universal fallbacks.

Reproducibility¶

Retain input and resolved configuration, actual models and versions, TT/UT1 values, raw astronomy, and derived results. Defaults must resolve to named choices before being recorded.

Results remain in memory; constructor checks verify pure derived-value consistency, not the claimed astronomical origin. A new natal calculation uses resolved UTC and the requested configuration without re-resolving timezones. Structured results, provenance and text output are described in the result guide.

Numerical agreement with celestial checks composition. Independent cases check the complete result under matched conventions. Tests must exercise timezone ambiguities, wrap, cusp ownership, rounding carry, retrograde motion, exact aspects, and undefined house geometry.

Delivery order¶

Birth/configuration values, the independent knowledge catalog, the tropical natal chart, major aspects and text output are implemented, with bilingual documentation and installed-package verification.

The v0.1.0 milestone owns task status. The source layout can evolve as these concrete implementations reveal shared needs; a general backend/plugin framework is not a prerequisite.
