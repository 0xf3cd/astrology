This development catalog is generated at documentation build time from the packaged records. All admitted concepts, variants, editions and entries appear below. Configuration links identify implemented request values, not astronomical algorithms. The historical variant has no executable implementation.

The knowledge guide explains queries, source scope, translation revisions and search limits. Original excerpts, project translations and authored statements are labeled separately. Retrieval never establishes chart applicability. The guide explains the source, prerequisite, output and chart states and translation revisions shown below.

rule.project.tropical_sign_sectors · Tropical sign sectors¶

The tropical zodiac divides ecliptic longitude into twelve half-open 30-degree sectors; a longitude exactly on a sector boundary belongs to the higher sign.

Project convention for the tropical zodiac; it is not a claim about the constellations or about historical sign division.

Applies to tropical longitudes only. The sidereal zodiac and the historical groupings in this catalog are not implemented.

Concepts / 概念：sign.aries, sign.taurus, sign.gemini, sign.cancer, sign.leo, sign.virgo, sign.libra, sign.scorpio, sign.sagittarius, sign.capricorn, sign.aquarius, sign.pisces

rule.project.cusp_ownership · Cusp ownership and wrap¶

House membership uses half-open intervals [cusp, next cusp): a longitude exactly on a cusp belongs to the house that starts there, and membership wraps across 0/360 degrees.

Project convention for two-dimensional house placement; the house systems themselves are named requests.

Uses the supplied cusp geometry only. Whether an axis coincides with a cusp is a property of the requested house system.

Concepts / 概念：house.whole_sign, house.equal, house.placidus

rule.project.aspect_deviation · Aspect deviation and branch¶

The oriented separation runs from the first object to the second, in (-180, 180] degrees of ecliptic longitude. An aspect with exact angle theta holds when the separation differs from a branch by at most the limit, the limit included. The branches are minus and plus theta for a sextile, square or trine, and the single angle 0 or 180 for a conjunction or opposition. Every holding branch is reported, in the order of the objects, then the aspect angle, then the minus branch before the plus branch.

Project convention; it is not attributed to a tradition.

Ecliptic longitude only. Latitude, declination, minor aspects, whole-sign aspects and aspects to axes are not evaluated.

Concepts / 概念：aspect.conjunction, aspect.sextile, aspect.square, aspect.trine, aspect.opposition

rule.project.aspect_motion · Applying and separating status¶

The relative longitude rate is the second object’s rate minus the first’s. Away from a deviation of 180 degrees, opposite signs of the relative rate and deviation mean applying; matching signs mean separating. Zero deviation is exact. Zero relative rate with nonzero deviation is stationary: the objects have no relative motion, but either planet may still be changing longitude. A missing rate is indeterminate. At a deviation of 180 degrees with nonzero relative motion, the absolute wrapped deviation has no two-sided derivative and the status is indeterminate. Floats are compared directly, without a tolerance.

Project definition; no tradition is claimed.

Describes this instant only. It does not predict whether an aspect will become exact; either object may change direction before that happens.

Concepts / 概念：aspect.conjunction, aspect.sextile, aspect.square, aspect.trine, aspect.opposition

rule.project.aspect_participation · Objects that take part in aspects¶

Aspects are judged among the requested objects, or among a chosen subset of them. Two lunar nodes never form an aspect with each other: the four node identities are one pair of nodes under two definitions and two directions. A node may form an aspect with a body. Axes are not objects of a chart request and take no part.

Project convention; it is not attributed to a tradition.

The exclusion of aspects between nodes is a project convention. A convention that draws no aspects for a node at all is expressed by leaving the node out of the chosen subset.

Concepts / 概念：aspect.conjunction, aspect.sextile, aspect.square, aspect.trine, aspect.opposition

rule.project.orb_either_within_own_orb · Either object’s own orb¶

Each object uses its separately assigned orb, or the aspect rule’s orb if none is assigned. The deviation must be within at least one object’s orb, so the limit is the larger of the two orbs.

Project definition; no tradition is claimed.

Needed exactly when an aspect rule gives per-object orbs. The catalog supplies no default orb.

entry.equinoctial_example · Statement / 陈述¶

The cited edition groups Aries and Libra as equinoctial signs. Inspecting this passage does not show that a particular chart satisfies any condition.

prerequisites=unresolved · output=reference_only · chart=not_assessed

Concepts / 概念：sign.aries, sign.libra

Variants / 变体：rule.ptolemy_ashmand.equinoctial_signs

Prerequisites / 前提：

  • A chart-level use would need an explicit zodiac convention, computed position and a separately specified use of this historical grouping; these are not evaluated here.

Exceptions / 例外：The historical rationale about equal day and night is preserved, not endorsed as an observed daylight guarantee.

Limits / 限度：Single-source, reference-only, non-executable example. Missing prerequisites remain unresolved; computing one condition cannot turn retrieval into full applicability.

source.ptolemy.ashmand1822: Book I, chapter XIV, printed pp. 34–35; PDF pages 72–73; complete equinoctial-sign sentence

Original excerpt (English) / 原文引述（英文）：

There are also two equinoctial signs: Aries, the first after the vernal equinox; and Libra, the first after the autumnal equinox: they are so called, because the Sun, when in the first point of either, makes the day and night equal.

分点星座也有两个：白羊座，春分之后的第一个；天秤座，秋分之后的第一个。它们如此命名，是因为太阳位于二者任一的起点时，使昼与夜等长。
