The tropical zodiac divides ecliptic longitude into twelve half-open 30-degree sectors; a longitude exactly on a sector boundary belongs to the higher sign.

Project convention for the tropical zodiac; it is not a claim about the constellations or about historical sign division.

rule.project.aspect_deviation · Aspect deviation and branch¶

The oriented separation runs from the first object to the second, in (-180, 180] degrees of ecliptic longitude. An aspect with exact angle theta holds when the separation differs from a branch by at most the limit, the limit included. The branches are minus and plus theta for a sextile, square or trine, and the single angle 0 or 180 for a conjunction or opposition. Every holding branch is reported, in the order of the objects, then the aspect angle, then the minus branch before the plus branch.

Project convention; it is not attributed to a tradition.

Ecliptic longitude only. Latitude, declination, minor aspects, whole-sign aspects and aspects to axes are not evaluated.

Concepts / 概念：aspect.conjunction, aspect.sextile, aspect.square, aspect.trine, aspect.opposition

rule.project.aspect_motion · Applying and separating status¶

For positions and rates at one shared instant, the relative longitude rate is the second object’s rate minus the first’s. Away from a deviation of 180 degrees, opposite signs of the relative rate and deviation mean applying; matching signs mean separating. Zero deviation is exact. Zero relative rate with nonzero deviation is stationary: the objects have no relative motion, but either planet may still be changing longitude. A missing rate is indeterminate. At a deviation of 180 degrees with nonzero relative motion, the absolute wrapped deviation has no two-sided derivative and the status is indeterminate. Floats are compared directly, without a tolerance.

Project definition; no tradition is claimed.

Describes this instant only. It does not predict whether an aspect will become exact; either object may change direction before that happens.

Concepts / 概念：aspect.conjunction, aspect.sextile, aspect.square, aspect.trine, aspect.opposition

rule.project.aspect_participation · Objects that take part in aspects¶

In single-chart evaluation, aspects are judged among the requested objects, or among a chosen subset of them. Two lunar nodes never form an aspect with each other: the four node identities are one pair of nodes under two definitions and two directions. A node may form an aspect with a body. Axes are not objects of a chart request and take no part.

Project convention; it is not attributed to a tradition.

The exclusion of aspects between nodes in single-chart evaluation is a project convention. A convention that draws no aspects for a node at all is expressed by leaving the node out of the chosen subset.

Concepts / 概念：aspect.conjunction, aspect.sextile, aspect.square, aspect.trine, aspect.opposition

rule.project.orb_either_within_own_orb · Either object’s own orb¶

Each object uses its separately assigned orb, or the aspect rule’s orb if none is assigned. The deviation must be within at least one object’s orb, so the limit is the larger of the two orbs.

Project definition; no tradition is claimed.

Needed exactly when an aspect rule gives per-object orbs. The catalog supplies no default orb.

rule.project.cross_aspect_evaluation · Static cross-chart aspect evaluation¶

Pair each selected left position with each selected right position, preserving both source charts. An omitted selection means all positions on that side, independently of natal participants. Same-named objects and all named node-node pairs are allowed, including the same chart in both roles. Use caller-supplied aspect rules and identity-keyed object orbs, never either chart’s natal rules. Retain every matching oriented branch, signed deviation and inclusive orb limit; no motion status is assigned.

Project geometry policy; no tradition or interpretation is claimed.

Compare matching tropical/geocentric own-date position conventions directly, without common-epoch conversion or a simultaneous spatial-angle claim. Rates at different birth instants do not establish shared evolution. No default orb, interpretation, house overlay or composite construction is supplied.

Concepts / 概念：aspect.conjunction, aspect.sextile, aspect.square, aspect.trine, aspect.opposition
