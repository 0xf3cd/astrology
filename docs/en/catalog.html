This catalog is generated at documentation build time from the packaged records. All admitted concepts, variants, editions and entries appear below. Configuration links identify implemented request values, not astronomical algorithms. The Ashmand 1822 table reads the executable sign-group definition; its historical evidence and limits remain distinct from chart applicability.

The knowledge guide explains queries, source scope, translation revisions and search limits. Original excerpts, project translations and authored statements are labeled separately. rule.project.solar_lunar_returns · Solar and lunar returns to natal longitude¶

A solar or lunar return is the moving Sun or Moon reaching the stored natal longitude of that same body. astrology.returns.return_hits selects a same-body conjunction through transit_hits and returns its TransitHits unchanged. The explicit half-open UTC interval selects all occurrences, including an empty result. Each retained hit can be passed to moment_chart with a caller-supplied location and ChartConfig.

Modern return definitions in Astrowiki, Mary Shea and Isabelle Ghaneh; the own-date apparent geocentric tropical comparison and explicit-location interface are project conventions. Birthplace and residence practices remain distinct choices made by the caller.

No common-epoch or sidereal transformation, calendar-anniversary shortcut or location inference. A calendar year can contain two solar returns; all events in the interval are retained. Search and chart construction are separate: a valid event need not admit the requested house geometry. Numerical reproducibility does not establish forecasting efficacy.

Concepts / 概念：body.sun, body.moon, aspect.conjunction

rule.lilly1647.essential_dignities · Lilly 1647 seven-planet rulership and four statuses¶

astrology.dignities owns the seven-planet domicile and sign-wide exaltation assignments. Detriment is opposite a domicile and fall opposite exaltation. essential_dignities returns all matches in domicile, exaltation, detriment, fall order; an empty tuple means none of these four. HouseRulers applies sign_ruler to each supplied cusp sign and retains existing ruler placements without recalculation; this composition policy is project-authored.

William Lilly, Christian Astrology (London, 1647), Book I, chapters VIII–XIV and XVIII. The table heading on p.104 spells the name Ptolomy; this spelling is retained when identifying Lilly’s table. Ptolemy’s original text was not inspected.

Only these four statuses for the seven classical planets. No exact-degree assessment, sect condition, node dignities, outer-planet rulers, triplicity, terms, faces, reception or scores. Having none of these four does not establish peregrine status. Chart composition reads actual cusps, not axes, preserves the selected system and leaves uncalculated ruler placements absent.

rule.project.aspect_deviation · Aspect deviation and branch¶

The oriented separation runs from the first object to the second, in (-180, 180] degrees of ecliptic longitude. An aspect with exact angle theta holds when the separation differs from a branch by at most the limit, the limit included. The branches are minus and plus theta for a sextile, square or trine, and the single angle 0 or 180 for a conjunction or opposition. Every holding branch is reported, in the order of the objects, then the aspect angle, then the minus branch before the plus branch.

Project convention; it is not attributed to a tradition.

Ecliptic longitude only. Latitude, declination, minor aspects and whole-sign aspects are not evaluated. Stored chart axes may participate when explicitly selected.

rule.project.aspect_motion · Applying and separating status¶

For positions and rates at one shared instant, the relative longitude rate is the second object’s rate minus the first’s. Away from a deviation of 180 degrees, opposite signs of the relative rate and deviation mean applying; matching signs mean separating. Zero deviation is exact. Zero relative rate with nonzero deviation is stationary: the objects have no relative motion, but either planet may still be changing longitude. A missing rate is indeterminate. At a deviation of 180 degrees with nonzero relative motion, the absolute wrapped deviation has no two-sided derivative and the status is indeterminate. Floats are compared directly, without a tolerance.

Project definition; no tradition is claimed.

Describes this instant only. It does not predict whether an aspect will become exact; either object may change direction before that happens.

Single-chart major aspects are judged from ecliptic longitudes and an explicit orb, with the oriented branch and a local applying or separating status kept in the result.

prerequisites=complete · output=reference_only · chart=not_assessed

Concepts / 概念：aspect.conjunction, aspect.sextile, aspect.square, aspect.trine, aspect.opposition

Prerequisites / 前提：

  • The caller supplies every orb; this entry gives none.

  • Per-object orbs need a chosen combination rule.

Exceptions / 例外：Two lunar nodes never form an aspect with each other in this single-chart evaluation.

Limits / 限度：This entry is a project definition, not an independent corroboration or a traditional rule. It supplies no default orb, strength or interpretation, and a retrieval never assesses a chart.

entry.cross_aspect_evaluation · Statement / 陈述¶

Static major cross aspects compare two supplied natal or moment results with distinct left/right endpoints and preserve both complete sources, without a motion field.

prerequisites=complete · output=reference_only · chart=not_assessed

Concepts / 概念：aspect.conjunction, aspect.sextile, aspect.square, aspect.trine, aspect.opposition

Variants / 变体：rule.project.cross_aspect_evaluation, rule.project.aspect_deviation, rule.project.orb_either_within_own_orb, rule.project.orb_both_within_own_orb, rule.project.orb_mean_of_orbs

Prerequisites / 前提：

  • Two supplied immutable natal or moment results with matching zodiac, center and position-frame conventions, plus explicit caller-supplied aspect rules.

  • An orb combination is required exactly when object overrides exist; overrides may name only selected identities from either side.

Exceptions / 例外：Same-named objects, node-node cross pairs, the same chart in both roles, empty selections and empty rules are admitted. The single-chart node exclusion does not apply.

Limits / 限度：Project-defined geometry, not independent corroboration or traditional interpretation. Own-date longitudes are not transformed to a common epoch. No native astronomy or time resolution is needed for comparison; catalog retrieval itself never evaluates charts.

entry.equinoctial_example · Statement / 陈述¶

The cited edition groups Aries and Libra as equinoctial signs. Inspecting this passage does not show that a particular chart satisfies any condition.

prerequisites=unresolved · output=reference_only · chart=not_assessed

Concepts / 概念：sign.aries, sign.libra

Variants / 变体：rule.ptolemy_ashmand.equinoctial_signs

Prerequisites / 前提：

  • A chart-level use would need an explicit zodiac convention, computed position and a separately specified use of this historical grouping; these are not evaluated here.

Exceptions / 例外：The historical rationale about equal day and night is preserved, not endorsed as an observed daylight guarantee.

Limits / 限度：Single-source, reference-only example with no implemented chart-level use. Pure sign-group lookup is available separately; its result cannot resolve the missing prerequisites or turn retrieval into full applicability.

entry.essential_dignities · Statement / 陈述¶

The named Lilly 1647 rule assigns sign rulers and retains every applicable domicile, exaltation, detriment and fall. Its tables are project expansions of inspected chapter assignments and opposite-sign definitions, not quotations of every printed table cell. Lilly’s zodiacal houses in these passages mean domiciles, not numbered chart houses.

prerequisites=complete · output=reference_only · chart=not_assessed

Concepts / 概念：body.sun, body.moon, body.mercury, body.venus, body.mars, body.jupiter, body.saturn, sign.aries, sign.taurus, sign.gemini, sign.cancer, sign.leo, sign.virgo, sign.libra, sign.scorpio, sign.sagittarius, sign.capricorn, sign.aquarius, sign.pisces, dignity_status.domicile, dignity_status.exaltation, dignity_status.detriment, dignity_status.fall

Variants / 变体：rule.lilly1647.essential_dignities

Prerequisites / 前提：

  • Supply an exact Sign and, for status assessment, a supported Body. HouseRulers requires a supplied NatalChart or MomentChart; existing placements are reused and absent ruler placements remain absent.

Exceptions / 例外：Wrong types and unsupported outer planets are rejected explicitly; supported no-match is an empty tuple. An uncalculated ruler placement is None, not no-match. Four-status emptiness is not a peregrine judgment.

Limits / 限度：One named historical set. No reception, aggregate strength, modern rulership or interpretation. The source’s day/night house labels and other dignities are not conditions on these four queries. Catalog retrieval never evaluates chart applicability.

entry.cross_house_overlays · Statement / 陈述¶

Directional house overlays preserve two supplied natal or moment results and distinguish an object’s owner from the chart whose houses receive it. Each side can select bodies, named lunar nodes and stored axes independently.

prerequisites=complete · output=reference_only · chart=not_assessed

Concepts / 概念：house.whole_sign, house.equal, house.placidus, axis.ascendant, axis.midheaven, axis.descendant, axis.imum_coeli

Variants / 变体：rule.project.cross_house_overlays, rule.project.cusp_ownership

Prerequisites / 前提：

  • Supply two immutable NatalChart or MomentChart results with matching zodiac, center and position-frame conventions and stored cusp geometry. Select objects independently, or use each source’s default bodies/nodes without axes.

Exceptions / 例外：Different house systems, dates, models and versions are retained. Same-named objects, the same chart in both roles and empty selections are allowed; an empty side still supplies its houses to the other direction.

Limits / 限度：Project geometry, without common-epoch conversion, three-dimensional placement or relationship interpretation. The fixed source documents implementation, not independent corroboration. Catalog retrieval does not evaluate charts or make this reference entry eligible for default interpretation output.

entry.axis_aspects · Statement / 陈述¶

Stored chart axes have distinct identities and participate in single-chart or static cross-chart aspects only when explicitly selected. Chart composition reads the corresponding axis fields, not house cusps or synthesized opposite axes.

prerequisites=complete · output=reference_only · chart=not_assessed

Concepts / 概念：axis.ascendant, axis.midheaven, axis.descendant, axis.imum_coeli

Variants / 变体：rule.project.aspect_participation, rule.project.aspect_deviation, rule.project.aspect_motion, rule.project.cross_aspect_evaluation

Prerequisites / 前提：

  • Supply explicit participants and aspect rules with caller-selected orbs. Chart composition requires stored axis geometry; cross-chart sources retain their own axis fields.

Exceptions / 例外：Axis-axis and opposite-axis pairs are admitted without folding identities. The single-chart node-node exclusion remains specific to lunar nodes; static cross-chart pairs have no motion field.

Limits / 限度：A project policy, not a universal traditional rule or astronomical authentication. Single-chart composition supplies no axis rate, so non-exact axis hits are indeterminate; zero deviation still gives exact. Scalar callers may supply a meaningful finite rate. Axes have no native-output target, physical distance, raw position or statistical placement. Catalog retrieval never evaluates chart applicability.

entry.dispositor_chains · Statement / 陈述¶

Dispositors retains the supplied chart and explicit roots, with immutable ruler edges, root paths, reached cycles, final dispositors and root-specific missing rulers. chart_dispositors constructs this result from existing placements.

prerequisites=complete · output=reference_only · chart=not_assessed

Concepts / 概念：body.sun, body.moon, body.mercury, body.venus, body.mars, body.jupiter, body.saturn, body.uranus, body.neptune, body.pluto, node.mean_ascending, node.mean_descending, node.meeus_five_term_true_ascending, node.meeus_five_term_true_descending

Variants / 变体：rule.project.dispositor_chains

Prerequisites / 前提：

  • Supply a NatalChart or MomentChart and a nonempty, duplicate-free sequence of available Body/LunarNode identities. Selection is copied into source-chart order; it selects roots, not the available intermediate rulers.

Exceptions / 例外：A missing successor remains an explicit identity without a fabricated placement. A root already in a cycle has no prefix before the cycle; several roots may share an endpoint or reach different components.

Limits / 限度：The source supports the unchanged sign-ruler table. Traversal, ordering and output conventions are project definitions, with the limits in rule.project.dispositor_chains. This entry supplies no score, interpretation or assessment of chart applicability.
