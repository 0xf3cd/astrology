class astrology.birth.ResolvedBirth(record, utc, utc_offset, timezone_key, fold, tzdata_version, iana_version)¶

A preserved record and separate UTC, offset, choice, and data provenance.

UTC requires tzinfo=datetime.UTC and fold=0, at or after 1972-01-01T00:00:00Z. IANA results carry both data versions; fixed offsets carry neither. Construction checks shape and preservation of the recorded clock as UTC plus offset, without loading timezone data to authenticate supplied offsets, folds or versions. resolve_birth_time validates the actual candidate and reads its data versions.

astrology.birth.resolve_birth_time(record, *, fold=None)¶

Resolve a known clock/timezone without requiring a location or changing the record.

IANA folds require 0 (earlier) or 1 (later); gaps and irrelevant choices fail. Read pinned tzdata only here. UTC before 1972 or outside datetime’s range fails. Unknown keys/malformed timezone data raise ValueError. Missing packages, invalid version metadata and remaining resource I/O failures are environment errors.

解析已知钟点和时区，不要求地点，也不改变记录。IANA 重叠时刻须选 0（较早）或 1（较晚）； 空缺时刻和无关选择均报错。仅在此处读取钉版 tzdata；早于 1972 或超出 datetime 范围的 UTC 报错。 未知标识或格式错误的时区数据抛出 ValueError；包缺失、版本元数据无效及其余资源 I/O 故障仍属环境错误。

class astrology.config.OrbCombination(*values)¶

Combine two objects’ orbs into one limit, without prescribing a tradition’s rule.

Each object has an orb for the aspect (its own override or the rule’s orb). A pair is within the limit when its deviation from the exact angle satisfies the rule: either object’s orb contains it (the larger orb), both objects’ orbs contain it (the smaller orb), or the mean of the two orbs contains it. A limit is inclusive.

将两个对象的容许度合成一个上限，不指定哪个规则属于哪个传统。每个对象使用单独设置的容许度， 未单独设置时使用相位规则的容许度。三种合成方式分别取较大值、较小值或平均值； 偏差的绝对值不超过该上限就算符合规则，等于上限也算。

class astrology.aspects.AspectMotion(*values)¶

Whether an aspect is approaching or moving away from its exact angle at this instant.

The relative longitude rate is the second object’s rate minus the first’s. Away from a deviation of 180 degrees, the deviation shrinks in magnitude when the relative rate and the deviation have opposite signs (applying) and grows when they have the same sign (separating). Statuses compare exact floats, with no tolerance. STATIONARY means the relative rate is exactly zero, not that either planet has stopped changing longitude (a station). INDETERMINATE means a rate is missing, or the deviation is 180 degrees with nonzero relative motion: its magnitude has no two-sided derivative there.

相位此刻在接近还是远离精确角度。相对黄经速率是第二个对象的速率减去第一个的速率。 偏差不为 180 度时，相对速率与偏差异号，偏差的绝对值在缩小（入相）；同号时在增大（离相）。 直接比较浮点数，不使用容差。STATIONARY 指相对速率恰为零， 不表示某颗行星的黄经速率为零（占星术语中的“留”）。 INDETERMINATE 指缺少速率，或偏差恰为 180 度且存在相对运动，此时偏差的绝对值没有双侧导数。

astrology.aspects.find_aspects(objects, rules, combination=None)¶

Return every aspect that holds between pairs of objects, in a fixed order.

Pairs follow the order of objects, then the aspect angle, then the branch (minus before plus), so the order is total. Every matching aspect and branch is returned: broad orbs may give one pair several hits, and none is dropped in favor of a nearer one. A hit holds when the deviation’s magnitude is at most the limit, with the limit itself included. The limit is the rule’s orb, or the combination of the two objects’ orbs when the rule has per-object orbs, which then requires combination.
