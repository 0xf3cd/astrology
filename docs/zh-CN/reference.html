Resolve a known clock/timezone without requiring a location or changing the record.

class astrology.config.OrbCombination(*values)

Combine two objects’ orbs into one limit, without prescribing a tradition’s rule.

Each object has an orb for the aspect (its own override or the rule’s orb). A pair is within the limit when its deviation from the exact angle satisfies the rule: either object’s orb contains it (the larger orb), both objects’ orbs contain it (the smaller orb), or the mean of the two orbs contains it. A limit is inclusive.

将两个对象的容许度合成一个上限，不指定哪个规则属于哪个传统。每个对象使用单独设置的容许度， 未单独设置时使用相位规则的容许度。三种合成方式分别取较大值、较小值或平均值； 偏差的绝对值不超过该上限就算符合规则，等于上限也算。

EITHER_WITHIN_OWN_ORB = 'either_within_own_orb'
BOTH_WITHIN_OWN_ORB = 'both_within_own_orb'
MEAN_OF_ORBS = 'mean_of_orbs'

exception astrology.chart.ChartDomainError(message, *, house_system, operation=None, recorded=None, upstream_message=None)

The request asks for a chart outside the supported computation domain.

Pre-call rejections (latitude at a pole, civil year 2100 or later) carry no upstream detail. Failures reported by the astronomy library carry the requested house system and the upstream operation, recording status and message. No partial chart is ever returned and no substitution occurs.

class astrology.chart.NatalChart(resolved, requested, effective, provenance, times, positions, houses, placements, aspects)

A complete immutable natal result; objects follow the requested order.

Aspects are those requested in requested.aspects, among the objects named in effective.aspect_objects, in the order documented by find_aspects. The positions, placements and aspects are copied into tuples, so lists given by the caller are not aliased.

astrology.chart.natal_chart(resolved, config)

Calculate a tropical, geocentric natal chart for a resolved birth instant.

The record must carry a location; unknown time was already rejected by preparation. The astronomy adapter is imported here, not at package import, so importing astrology or querying knowledge never initializes native astronomy. A requested geometry outside the joint upstream domain raises ChartDomainError and never falls back to another house system. Requested aspect rules are judged among the objects of config.aspect_objects, or among all requested objects when it is unset, from the positions and longitude rates of this calculation. The distribution must be installed: missing package metadata raises importlib.metadata.PackageNotFoundError rather than inventing a version.

为已解析的出生时刻计算热带地心本命盘。出生记录必须带地点；时间未知的记录在准备阶段 就已被拒绝。天文适配层在这个函数里才导入，不在导入包时导入，所以导入 astrology 或查询 知识都不会初始化原生天文库。请求的几何超出上游的联合求值域时，抛出 ChartDomainError，不会改用别的宫制。根据这次计算的位置和黄经速率， 在 config.aspect_objects 指定的对象之间计算相位；未设置时，所有请求的对象都参与。 分发包必须已安装；元数据缺失时抛出 importlib.metadata.PackageNotFoundError，不编造版本号。

astrology.serialization.loads_chart(data)

Restore schema v1 without astronomy, timezone lookup or replacing stored values.

Reject unknown/missing/duplicate fields, unsupported identities, nonfinite values, noncanonical ISO timestamps and inconsistent pure derivations with ChartRecordError. 相位¶

Find major aspects from longitudes and rates at a given instant.

Each result records the angle measured from the first object to the second, the exact aspect angle used for comparison (the branch), and the signed difference. The motion status tells whether the magnitude of that difference is shrinking or growing. It does not predict later changes in motion or whether the aspect will become exact. This module needs no chart and does not call the astronomy library.

根据同一时刻的黄经和速率判断主要相位。每条结果记录从第一个对象到第二个对象的角度、 用来对照的精确相位角（分支），以及两者的带符号偏差。运动状态说明偏差的绝对值此刻在缩小还是增大， 不预测之后的运动变化，也不预测相位之后会不会变得精确。本模块不需要命盘，也不调用天文库。

class astrology.aspects.AspectMotion(*values)

Whether an aspect is approaching or moving away from its exact angle at this instant.

The relative longitude rate is the second object’s rate minus the first’s. Away from a deviation of 180 degrees, the deviation shrinks in magnitude when the relative rate and the deviation have opposite signs (applying) and grows when they have the same sign (separating). Statuses compare exact floats, with no tolerance. STATIONARY means the relative rate is exactly zero, not that either planet has stopped changing longitude (a station). INDETERMINATE means a rate is missing, or the deviation is 180 degrees with nonzero relative motion: its magnitude has no two-sided derivative there.

相位此刻在接近还是远离精确角度。相对黄经速率是第二个对象的速率减去第一个的速率。 偏差不为 180 度时，相对速率与偏差异号，偏差的绝对值在缩小（入相）；同号时在增大（离相）。 直接比较浮点数，不使用容差。STATIONARY 指相对速率恰为零， 不表示某颗行星的黄经速率为零（占星术语中的“留”）。 INDETERMINATE 指缺少速率，或偏差恰为 180 度且存在相对运动，此时偏差的绝对值没有双侧导数。

astrology.aspects.find_aspects(objects, rules, combination=None)

Return every aspect that holds between pairs of objects, in a fixed order.

Pairs follow the order of objects, then the aspect angle, then the branch (minus before plus), so the order is total. Every matching aspect and branch is returned: broad orbs may give one pair several hits, and none is dropped in favor of a nearer one. A hit holds when the deviation’s magnitude is at most the limit, with the limit itself included. The limit is the rule’s orb, or the combination of the two objects’ orbs when the rule has per-object orbs, which then requires combination. Two lunar nodes never form an aspect: the four identities are one pair of nodes under two definitions and two directions.
